## Thirty-minute session

Allow five minutes per fan and five minutes to compare notes. Start with past behaviour before showing the product.

1. What decision did you last argue about, and what did you do next?
2. What information would have helped you understand the consequences?
3. Show the viewer. Ask them to find a moment and explore an alternative without guidance.
4. What do they think the two timelines and probability lines mean?
5. Would they open this during play, at halftime, or after the match? What would make them distrust it?

Record an anonymous participant code, the actual incident they recall, their next action, whether they completed the task unaided, and whether they understood the uncertainty. Ask permission before recording anyone. Do not include names or contact details in this public repository.
